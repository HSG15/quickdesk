# Employee Personal Details and Identity Document Update Policy

**Article ID:** KB-HR-003  
**Category:** HR  
**Last Updated:** 2026-02-15  

## Overview
All full-time and contract employees must ensure their statutory records, residential address, and government identification details (Aadhaar Card, PAN, Passport) are kept accurate in the company Human Resources Management System (HRMS) for payroll, tax filing, and statutory compliance.

## Updating Address and Identity Documents (Aadhaar / PAN / Passport)
1. **Accessing the HR Portal**:
   - Navigate to `https://hr.company.com/profile` and log in with your single sign-on (SSO) credentials.
   - Go to **My Profile** > **Personal & Statutory Details** > **Address / Government IDs**.

2. **Aadhaar Card & Permanent Address Updates**:
   - Enter your updated 12-digit Aadhaar number and the new residential address matching the official record.
   - Upload a clear scanned PDF or JPG copy of the updated masked Aadhaar Card (first 8 digits masked) showing the current address.
   - Alternatively, valid proof of address includes a recent electricity bill (< 3 months old), signed lease/rental agreement, or bank account statement.

3. **Tax & PAN Card Updates**:
   - Ensure your name on company records exactly matches the name on your Income Tax PAN database to prevent TDS deduction discrepancies.
   - For corrections, upload a digital copy of the re-issued PAN card under the **Tax Compliance** section.

## Verification & Processing Timeline
- **HR Review SLA**: The People Operations team reviews and verifies all identity and address submissions within **2 business days**.
- **Payroll Synchronization**: Changes approved before the 20th of the current calendar month reflect in that month's payroll pay slip and tax declarations.
- **Support Contact**: For urgent corrections or name-change legal affidavits, contact `hr-operations@quickdesk.com` or ping the internal `#hr-support` Slack channel.
