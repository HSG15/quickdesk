# Employee Personal Details and Identity Document Update Policy

**Article ID:** KB-HR-003  
**Category:** HR  
**Last Updated:** 2026-10-25

## How to update details
1. Open **https://hr.company.com/profile** and sign in with company SSO.
2. Go to **My Profile > Personal & Statutory Details > Address / Government IDs**.
3. Update the required fields and upload the requested proof.
4. For Aadhaar proof, use a masked copy with the first eight digits hidden. Accepted address proof may include an electricity bill less than 3 months old, signed lease, or bank statement.
5. For PAN corrections, upload the re-issued PAN in **Tax Compliance**. Your name should match the Income Tax PAN database.

## Review and timing
- People Operations reviews submissions within **2 business days**.
- Changes approved before the **20th of the month** are expected to appear in that month's payslip and tax declarations.
- For urgent legal name corrections, contact **hr-operations@quickdesk.com** or **#hr-support** on Slack.

Never post full identity numbers or identity-document images in a public channel or ordinary ticket. Use the HR portal.

## Quick FAQs
- **Address update:** HR portal > My Profile > Personal & Statutory Details.
- **Review SLA:** 2 business days.
- **Same-month payroll:** Approval before the 20th.

## Company events and annual function
This section is general guidance for finding event information; it does not define an actual event date, venue, budget, or mandatory attendance rule.

- Check the official company calendar and HR announcement for the date, time, venue or meeting link, registration deadline, and attendance instructions.
- RSVP by the deadline if requested. If you cannot attend an event marked mandatory, follow the invitation and inform your manager.
- Do not assume travel, meals, accommodation, guest entry, or reimbursement are included unless the invitation confirms it.
- Get approval before making purchases. Expense claims must follow the expense reimbursement policy.
- Contact the event organizer or HR privately for accessibility or dietary requirements.

**Annual function FAQ:** The date and venue are not specified in this knowledge base. Check the official calendar or HR announcement.
