# Annual Leave and Sick Leave Policy

**Article ID:** KB-HR-001  
**Category:** HR  
**Last Updated:** 2026-01-10  

## Overview
QuickDesk supports employee work-life balance and health through structured paid time off (PTO).

## Leave Entitlements
- **Annual Paid Leave:** 20 business days per calendar year accrued monthly.
- **Paid Sick Leave:** 10 days per year for illness, medical appointments, or caregiving.
- **Bereavement Leave:** 5 consecutive business days for immediate family members.
- **Parental Leave:** 16 weeks of fully paid leave for primary caregivers.

## How to Apply for Leave
1. Log in to the HR Portal at `https://hr.company.com/leave`.
2. Select your leave type and specify the date range.
3. For leaves longer than 3 business days, submit your request at least 2 weeks in advance to allow workload coverage.
4. Your manager has a 48-hour SLA to approve or request rescheduling.

## Carry-Over Policy
- Up to 5 unused annual leave days can be carried over into the next calendar year.
- Carried-over days must be utilized before March 31st of the new year, or they expire automatically without cash encashment.
