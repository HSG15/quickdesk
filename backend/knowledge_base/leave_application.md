# Employee Leave Policy: Annual, Vacation, Sick, Casual & Other Leave

**Article ID:** KB-HR-001  
**Category:** HR  
**Last Updated:** 2026-10-25

> **Policy note:** Annual, sick, bereavement, parental, application, approval, and carry-over rules below come from the supplied original article. The casual-leave allowance is an illustrative demo rule because the original file did not define casual leave. Confirm actual casual-leave rules with HR.

## Leave types
- **Annual / vacation leave:** 20 business days per calendar year, accrued monthly.
- **Sick leave:** 10 days per year for illness, medical appointments, or caregiving.
- **Casual leave (demo only):** Up to 6 days per calendar year, subject to HRMS balance and manager approval. This is not a verified company entitlement.
- **Bereavement leave:** 5 consecutive business days for an immediate family member's death.
- **Parental leave:** 16 weeks of fully paid leave for primary caregivers.
- The source does not define public holidays, half-days, unpaid leave, or casual/sick leave carry-over. Ask HR.

## How to apply
1. Visit **https://hr.company.com/leave**.
2. Select the leave type and date range; check your balance.
3. For more than **3 business days**, apply at least **2 weeks in advance**.
4. The manager has **48 hours** to approve or request rescheduling.
5. Track the request in HRMS. Submission does not mean approval.

For unexpected illness, notify your manager as soon as practical and submit the HRMS request when able. The source does not specify when a medical certificate is required.

## Carry-over
Up to **5 unused annual-leave days** can be carried into the next calendar year and must be used before **March 31** or they expire without cash encashment. Other leave carry-over rules are not specified.

## Quick FAQs
- **Annual leave:** 20 business days/year.
- **Sick leave:** 10 days/year.
- **Casual leave:** Demo-only allowance of up to 6 days/year; confirm with HR.
- **Apply for more than 3 days:** At least 2 weeks ahead.
- **Manager response:** Within 48 hours.
- **Annual leave carry-over:** Up to 5 days, used by March 31.
