# Password Reset Policy & Self-Service Portal

**Article ID:** KB-IT-002  
**Category:** IT  
**Last Updated:** 2026-10-25

## Password requirements
- Minimum **14 characters**.
- Include uppercase, lowercase, number, and special character (`!@#$%^&*`).
- Cannot match any of the previous **5 passwords**.
- Passwords expire every **90 days** under the supplied policy.

## Reset your password
1. Visit **https://sso.company.com/recovery**.
2. Enter your corporate email.
3. Verify using the registered SMS code or authenticator app.
4. Set a new password that meets the requirements.
5. Allow up to **5 minutes** for synchronization across Google Workspace, Slack, and Jira.

## If you cannot reset
If you cannot access your registered phone or authenticator, submit an IT ticket or call the Helpdesk Hotline. Identity verification requires oral confirmation from your direct manager by video call or a signed Slack confirmation.

Never share passwords or verification codes in a ticket. If reset succeeds but an application still fails, provide the app name, reset time, and exact error.

## Quick FAQs
- **Reset portal:** https://sso.company.com/recovery
- **Sync time:** Up to 5 minutes.
- **No recovery phone:** Contact Helpdesk; manager verification is required.
