# Password Reset Policy & Self-Service Portal

**Article ID:** KB-IT-002  
**Category:** IT  
**Last Updated:** 2026-02-01  

## Overview
All employees must maintain strong, unique passwords to safeguard corporate systems. Passwords expire automatically every 90 days.

## Password Complexity Requirements
- Minimum length of 14 characters.
- Must include at least one uppercase letter (A-Z).
- Must include at least one lowercase letter (a-z).
- Must include at least one numeric digit (0-9).
- Must include at least one special character (!@#$%^&*).
- Cannot match any of your previous 5 passwords.

## Self-Service Password Reset (SSPR)
1. Navigate to the self-service portal: `https://sso.company.com/recovery`.
2. Enter your corporate email and verify your identity via the registered SMS code or authenticator app.
3. Enter your new password and confirm.
4. Allow up to 5 minutes for password synchronization across Google Workspace, Slack, and Jira.

## Emergency Password Unlock
If you cannot access your recovery phone or authenticator app:
- Submit an IT ticket or call the Helpdesk Hotline.
- Identity verification requires oral confirmation from your direct manager via video call or a signed Slack confirmation message.
