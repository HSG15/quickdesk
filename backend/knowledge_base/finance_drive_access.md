# Finance Shared Drive Permissions & Security Policy

**Article ID:** KB-FIN-001  
**Category:** Finance  
**Last Updated:** 2026-10-25

## Requesting access
The Finance Shared Drive (`\\corp.internal\finance\shared`) contains sensitive financial data. Request only the folders needed for your role.

1. Email your direct manager with the business reason and exact subfolders required (for example, `Billing`, `Payroll_Reports`, or `Accounts_Payable`).
2. The manager forwards the request to **finance-approvals@company.com** for Head of Finance review.
3. After written approval, open a QuickDesk ticket under **Finance** and attach the approval email as a PDF or screenshot.
4. IT Systems provisions approved access within **24 hours** after receiving the complete request.

## Security
- Do not download confidential spreadsheets to unmanaged, non-corporate devices.
- Do not share Finance files with people who lack approved access.
- Access is audited quarterly. Permissions with no access for **60 days** are automatically revoked.
- If approved access still fails, include the folder path, approval date, device, exact error, and ticket number. Do not attach confidential file contents.

## Quick FAQs
- **How to request:** Manager approval → Head of Finance approval → Finance QuickDesk ticket with proof.
- **Provisioning time:** Within 24 hours of a complete approved request.
- **Personal laptop downloads:** Not allowed for confidential spreadsheets.
