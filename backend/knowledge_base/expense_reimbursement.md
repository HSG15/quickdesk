# Employee Expense Reimbursement Policy

**Article ID:** KB-FIN-002  
**Category:** Finance  
**Last Updated:** 2026-02-15  

## Overview
This policy outlines permissible business expenses and guidelines for prompt reimbursement.

## Eligible Business Expenses
- Client entertainment meals (capped at $75 per person).
- Approved business travel: economy flights, standard hotel rooms, airport transfers.
- Mobile phone and home internet stipend ($50/month for remote employees).
- Professional development: books, certifications, and conferences pre-approved by your department head.

## Ineligible Expenses
- Traffic violations, parking tickets, or towing fines.
- Personal entertainment (in-flight movies, hotel minibars, gym passes).
- Alcoholic beverages unless pre-approved for formal client hospitality events.

## Submission Guidelines & Deadlines
1. Submit expense reports through Expensify (`https://expenses.company.com`) within 30 days of the transaction date.
2. Itemized receipts are mandatory for all transactions exceeding $25. Credit card summary slips are not accepted.
3. Expenses exceeding $500 require approval from both your direct manager and department VP.
4. Approved expense reimbursements are processed via direct deposit on the 15th and 30th of each month.
