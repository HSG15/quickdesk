# Employee Expense Reimbursement Policy

**Article ID:** KB-FIN-002  
**Category:** Finance  
**Last Updated:** 2026-10-25

## Eligible expenses
- Client-entertainment meals: maximum $75 per person.
- Approved business travel: economy flights, standard hotel rooms, airport transfers.
- Mobile phone and home-internet stipend: $50/month for eligible remote employees.
- Books, certifications, and conferences pre-approved by the department head.

## Not eligible
Traffic violations, parking/towing fines, personal entertainment (such as hotel minibars or gym passes), and alcohol unless pre-approved for formal client hospitality.

## How to claim
1. Submit through Expensify: **https://expenses.company.com** within **30 days** of the transaction.
2. Itemized receipts are required for transactions **over $25**. Credit-card summary slips are not accepted as receipts.
3. Expenses **over $500** need approval from both the direct manager and department VP.
4. Approved reimbursements are processed by direct deposit on the **15th and 30th** of each month.

If a claim is rejected, follow the reviewer comments and resubmit. If payment is delayed, contact Finance with the report ID and submission date. Do not include full bank details in a ticket.

## Quick FAQs
- **Deadline:** 30 days from transaction.
- **Receipt threshold:** Itemized receipt for expenses over $25.
- **Approval over $500:** Direct manager and department VP.
- **Payment schedule:** 15th and 30th of each month.
- **Missing receipt:** The policy does not define an exception; ask Finance.
